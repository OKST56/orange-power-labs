PROJECT 03 / RECOMPILATION RESEARCH

NCAA Football 07 Recomp

Bringing a childhood football favorite toward native Windows, with a Missouri high school football conversion as the long-term vision.

THE ORIGIN STORY

A childhood favorite.
A new possibility.

NCAA Football 14 is my favorite video game of all time. But growing up, NCAA Football 07 held that spot for years.

Learning about original Xbox static recompilation and xboxrecomp opened up an exciting possibility: could one of my childhood favorites become a native Windows game?

That question sparked a new project under Orange Power Labs. First comes the challenge of bringing NCAA Football 07 to PC. The long-term dream is to turn that foundation into a Missouri high school football experience.

ORIGINAL XBOX → NATIVE WINDOWS

Research in progress.

Framework: xboxrecomp. Tools: Codex and VS Code, with Claude Code under consideration for primary development work.

Full executable linking, a successful game boot, and playable football have not been demonstrated. There is no public build or release date.

THE DEVELOPMENT APPROACH

One verified step at a time.

Development follows the pinned xboxrecomp repository’s README, Quick Start, getting-started documentation, and official pipeline instructions.
