NCAA FOOTBALL 14 / PLAYSTATION 3

Research before expansion.

The goal is to bring Missouri high school programs into NCAA Football 14, expand supported team counts, and implement Missouri high school playoff structures.

Early work includes establishing the workspace, collecting and verifying three EBOOT variants, and making initial structural comparisons. STEP-003 verification completed with nine passing tests, followed by STEP-004 provenance investigation.

Analysis compares the original executable with College Football Revamped and Forever14 to understand executable modifications and team expansion. Tools include Codex, VS Code, RPCS3, and executable and database analysis.
